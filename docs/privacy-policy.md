# Privacy Policy — Kids Photo Viewer

Last updated: 2026-09-05

Kids Photo Viewer does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal
data.

## What the app accesses

The app asks for permission to read the photos and videos already on your
device, so it can display them as a slideshow. It reads them directly from
the device's own media library and shows them on screen. Nothing is copied,
uploaded, modified or deleted.

## No network access

The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere, and it
works with the device fully offline.

## No accounts, no analytics, no ads

There is no sign-in, no user account, no analytics or tracking SDK, no
advertising, and no third-party service of any kind.

## Crash information

If the app crashes, the technical error message is saved in the app's own
private storage and shown to you the next time you open the app, so a problem
can be described in a bug report. It is deleted as soon as it is shown, and it
is never sent anywhere.

## Children

The app is a tool for parents and carers who want to hand their own device to
a child. It contains no content of its own — it shows only the photos and
videos that are already on the device.

## Deleting your data

The app stores nothing beyond the crash message described above. Uninstalling
the app removes it. Your photos and videos are untouched.
